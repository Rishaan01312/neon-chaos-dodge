MORE INFO

Powerups

🛡 Shield
Grants invulnerability when collected, and is stackable. Use to survive tight windows and avoid death penalties. Drop chance: 50%.

⏱ Slow Time
Temporarily reduces obstacle speed and spawn rate for smoother navigation. Useful for recovery. Drop chance: 50%.

×2 Multiplier
Doubles score & coin gains while active. Stacking multipliers increases pacing for high-score runs. Drop chance: 50%.

×3 Multiplier
Triples score & coin gains for a short burst — rarer but high payoff. Drop chance: 15%.

Coin Bag
Instant coin payout. Best when near a shop purchase or to meet prestige requirements. Drop chance: 5%.

Skins

Celestial
Passive: score boost + coin magnet on pick-up. Rare (0.3%).

Phantom
Active: temporary invulnerability to obstacles while ability is used. Rare (0.2%).

Void
Active: clears visible obstacles on activation. Ultra-rare (0.1%).

Prestige Boosts

Coin Spawn Rate
Each +5% reduces the interval between coin spawns (minimum cap), increasing the number of coins you can collect per run. Stacks additively.

Coin Value
Each +5% increases the value of every coin. Since coin rewards are integers, values are rounded up to preserve meaningful gains.

Score Gain
Each +5% multiplies per-action score gain, helping you reach higher scores faster.

Money Bag Reward
Each +5% increases the payout from money bag drops, making rare bag drops more impactful.

Speed Progress
Each +5% reduces the score required to reach the next speed level, accelerating progression to higher difficulty and reward tiers.

Other

Chaos Mode
A harder mode that increases obstacle speed and spawn rate while boosting score gain — toggle in settings for challenge runs.

Prestige
A full reset that grants prestige tokens when requirements are met. Tokens can be spent permanently on the Prestige Shop to buy +5% boosts that persist across resets (excluding hard resets).
